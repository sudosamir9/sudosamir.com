## Predicted weak spots before any data exists

Until a real attempt fills this in, these are the areas that most commonly cost people this exam, based on the blueprint weights and on what the available study material under-serves. Treat this as a starting hypothesis to disprove, not as a finding.

- **3.6, transaction versus stats.** The heaviest section is 15% and its hardest objective is a judgement call rather than a fact. Neither the Udemy course nor the Apress book does the comparison.
- **10.0, CIM.** 10% of the exam with no official Splunk course behind it. Most candidates arrive knowing that CIM normalizes field names and nothing more specific.
- **7.3 and 7.4, macro arguments.** The `name(n)` argument-count naming and `$arg$` substitution are tested character by character, and the course only shows a zero-argument macro.
- **8.3, POST workflow actions.** Named everywhere, demonstrated almost nowhere.
- **5.0 versus 4.0 ordering.** What a calculated field can and cannot see. This is really a question about search-time precedence wearing a different hat.
- **2.3, fillnull.** A named objective with its own bullet that the course skips entirely.
